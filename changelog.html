Changelog

What is in each release of Procrastop. This page is updated when a version reaches the App Store.

Version 1.0.0

The list

  • One list in three timeframes, Short-Term, Mid-Term and Long-Term, ordered by how close each due date is. Overdue tasks stay in Short-Term and keep counting.
  • Term lengths you set yourself, so each timeframe covers the number of days that suits you.
  • Focus Mode, showing only the most urgent tasks per timeframe and folding the rest behind a More Tasks strip.
  • Tap the plus button for the full task form with a calendar due date. Hold it for Quick Add when a title and a date are enough.
  • Notes, and repeating tasks, daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
  • Swipe a task to complete or edit it.
  • Finished Tasks, where a completed task can be restored or removed for good.

Reminders

  • Reminders for tasks due today, for tasks moving into a nearer timeframe, and at an exact due time, plus a task count on the app icon.
  • Annoy Me!, which repeats every hour until the task is finished.
  • Bombard Me!, deciding whether those hourly reminders stack in Notification Center or replace one another.
  • Nag Me!, a system alarm that sounds at the due time even in silent mode and returns every five minutes until the task is done. Available on iPhones running iOS 26 or later.
  • Provoke Me!, adding a line of encouragement under the task name, with an off switch.
  • A choice of tone for notifications and a separate one for the Nag Me! alarm.
  • Mark a task done or push it an hour later straight from a reminder, without opening the app.

Everything else

  • Export and Backup of every task as JSON and CSV, emailed to yourself.
  • System, Light or Dark appearance, and haptics you can turn off.
  • Three optional tips in Settings for anyone who wants to support the work. They unlock nothing.
  • No account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
