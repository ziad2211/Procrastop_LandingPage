Changelog

What is in each release of Procrastop. This page is updated when a version reaches the App Store.

Version 1.0.0

The first release. It includes:

  • One list in three timeframes — Short-Term, Mid-Term, and Long-Term — ordered by how close each due date is, with overdue tasks kept in Short-Term.
  • Term lengths you set yourself, so each timeframe covers the number of days that suits you.
  • Focus Mode, showing only the most urgent tasks per timeframe and collapsing the rest behind a “More … Tasks” strip.
  • Tap + for the full task form with a calendar due date; hold + for Quick Add.
  • Due times with a dial-based time screen, and Bother Me for hourly reminders until a task is finished.
  • Notes, stars, and repeating tasks — daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
  • Swipe a task left to complete, star, or edit it.
  • Finished Tasks, where a completed task can be restored or deleted for good.
  • Local reminders for tasks due today, for tasks moving into a nearer timeframe, and at an exact due time, plus a task count on the app icon.
  • Export / Backup of every task as JSON and CSV, emailed to yourself.
  • System, Light, or Dark appearance.
  • No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking — everything stays on your iPhone.
