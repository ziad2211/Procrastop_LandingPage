Frequently asked questions

What does Procrastop do?

Every task you add has a due date, and that date decides where it sits. Procrastop keeps one list split into three sections — Short-Term, Mid-Term, and Long-Term — and a task moves up on its own as its deadline gets closer. You never drag, reorder, or file anything; you just finish what is at the top.

How are the three timeframes decided?

By how many days away the due date is. The day ranges are yours to set in Settings › Change Term Lengths, so “Short-Term” can mean the next two days or the next week, whichever matches how you work.

What happens to a task I miss?

It stays in Short-Term and keeps counting, showing how many days overdue it is. Nothing is hidden or silently archived.

What is Focus Mode?

With Focus Mode on, each section shows only its most urgent tasks and collapses the rest behind a “More … Tasks” strip. Tap the strip or drag it down when you want the whole list back. You can turn it off, or change how many tasks stay visible, in Settings.

How do I add a task?

Tap the + button for the full form — title, due date on a calendar, due time, note, repeat, and star. Hold the + button instead for Quick Add when you only want a title and a date.

What does “Bother Me” do?

For a task with a due time, Bother Me keeps reminding you every hour until you mark the task finished. It is off unless you switch it on for that task.

Why does Procrastop ask for notifications?

Reminders are the whole point of a deadline, and they are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone: a nudge for what is due today, a note when a task moves into a nearer timeframe, exact-time reminders, and the count on the app icon. Decline the permission and the app still works normally — you simply will not be reminded. You can change your mind any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Procrastop.

Can I get a finished task back?

Yes. Completed tasks move to Settings › Finished Tasks; tap one to restore it to your list.

Do I need an account?

No. Procrastop has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud. Nothing is synced between devices, and your tasks stay on the iPhone that created them.

Where is my data stored, and can I get it out?

Your tasks and settings live in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Settings › Export / Backup produces a JSON and CSV copy of everything and attaches it to an email addressed to you, or hands it to the share sheet if Mail is not set up. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual tasks in the app, or delete Procrastop from your iPhone to remove everything it stored. See Delete your data.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Procrastop is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no tracking.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, running iOS 16.4 or later, in portrait. There is no iPad or Android version.

Can I add projects, tags, or calendar sync?

No, and that is deliberate. Procrastop is due dates, three timeframes, and reminders — nothing else to maintain.

How do I get Procrastop?

Search the App Store for “Procrastop”.

How can I report a problem?
