Frequently asked questions

What does Procrastop actually do?

Every task has a due date, and that date decides where it sits. The list is split into Short-Term, Mid-Term and Long-Term, and a task moves up on its own as its deadline gets closer. You never drag, reorder or file anything. The second half of the app is about reminders: you decide, task by task, how insistent they should be.

How are the three timeframes decided?

By how many days away the due date is. The ranges are yours to set in Settings, Change Term Lengths, so Short-Term can mean the next two days or the next week, whichever matches how you work.

What happens to a task I miss?

It stays in Short-Term and keeps counting, showing how many days overdue it is. Nothing is hidden or quietly archived.

What is Focus Mode?

With Focus Mode on, each section shows only its most urgent tasks and folds the rest behind a More Tasks strip. Tap the strip when you want the whole list back. You can turn it off, or change how many tasks stay visible, in Settings.

What is the difference between Annoy Me! and Nag Me!?

Annoy Me! is a notification that comes back every hour until the task is finished. Nag Me! is a system alarm rather than a notification: it sounds at the due time even when your iPhone is on silent, and every action on it gives you five more minutes before it returns. Annoy Me! is for the task you will get to. Nag Me! is for the one you will not.

A single task uses one or the other, not both. Both are off until you turn them on, and both can be switched off for the whole app in Settings, Notification Options.

What does Bombard Me! do?

It changes how Annoy Me! reminders stack up. With it on, every hourly reminder stays in Notification Center, so the pile grows until you deal with the task. With it off, each new reminder replaces the last and a task never shows more than once.

What is Provoke Me!?

It adds a line of encouragement under the task name in reminders. Turn it off and a reminder is just the task and when it is due.

Can I change the sounds?

Yes. Settings, Notification Options has one sound for ordinary notifications, including Annoy Me!, and a separate one for the Nag Me! alarm. Tap any tone to hear it before choosing it.

Can I finish a task from the reminder?

Yes. Press and hold a reminder to mark the task done or push it an hour down the road, without opening the app.

Why does Procrastop ask for notifications?

Reminders are the point of a deadline. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone: a nudge for what is due today, a note when a task moves into a nearer timeframe, reminders at an exact due time, and the count on the app icon. Decline the permission and the app still works normally, you simply will not be reminded. You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings, Notifications, Procrastop. Nag Me! asks separately, because alarms are their own permission.

Can I get a finished task back?

Yes. Completed tasks move to Settings, Finished Tasks. Tap one to restore it to your list.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud. Nothing syncs between devices, and your tasks stay on the iPhone that created them.

Where is my data stored, and can I get it out?

Your tasks and settings live in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Settings, Export and Backup produces a JSON and CSV copy of everything and attaches it to an email addressed to you. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual tasks in the app, or delete Procrastop from your iPhone to remove everything it stored. See Delete your data.

What does it cost? Are there ads?

Procrastop is $0.99 once, on the App Store. There is no subscription, no ads and no tracking, and nothing inside the app is locked behind a further payment. Settings offers three optional tips as In-App Purchases for anyone who wants to support the work, but they unlock nothing and the app is identical whether or not you ever send one.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, running iOS 16.4 or later, in portrait. The Nag Me! alarm relies on a newer system feature and appears only on iPhones running iOS 26 or later. There is no iPad or Android version.

Can I add projects, tags or calendar sync?

No, and that is deliberate. Procrastop is due dates, three timeframes and reminders. There is nothing else to maintain.

How can I report a problem?

Email ziadmohofficial@gmail.com with your app version, iPhone model and iOS version. See Support for what helps most.
