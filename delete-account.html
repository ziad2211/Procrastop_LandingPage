Delete your data

There is no account to delete. Procrastop has no sign-in and no server. Your tasks and settings exist only in the app's storage on your iPhone, and the developer never receives them. Removing them is entirely in your hands.

Delete individual tasks

  1. Open Procrastop.
  2. Tap the task you want to remove.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the task and tap Delete Task.
  4. Confirm. The task is removed immediately. It does not go to Finished Tasks and cannot be restored.

Delete finished tasks

  1. Open Settings, then Finished Tasks.
  2. Remove a single finished task, or clear the whole list at once.

Delete everything

  1. Press and hold the Procrastop icon on your Home Screen.
  2. Choose Remove App, then Delete App.

What happens next

Deleting a task removes it straight away. Deleting the app removes its whole storage, meaning every task, finished task and setting, along with any reminders and alarms it had scheduled. Because nothing was ever uploaded, there is no copy elsewhere for the developer to erase and no request you need to send.

One thing sits outside the app's control. If you previously made an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, that backup may still contain the app's data until it is replaced or deleted in your iOS or iCloud settings. The same is true of any backup file you emailed yourself through Settings, Export and Backup.
