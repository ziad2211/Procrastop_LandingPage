Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version. Procrastop has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Everything you write stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. The developer cannot see your tasks, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind in the app.

Who this policy is from

Procrastop is made and published by Ziad Ismail. Questions about this policy go to ziadmohofficial@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your iPhone

All of the following is written to the app's own storage on your device. None of it is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

  • Your tasks. Titles, due dates, optional due times, notes, repeat settings, whether a task uses Annoy Me! or Nag Me!, and completion history.
  • Your settings. Timeframe lengths, Focus Mode, notification and alarm preferences, chosen alert sounds, appearance, haptics and the app icon badge.
  • A small amount of app bookkeeping. A count of how many tasks you have completed, used only to decide when to offer the App Store rating prompt once, and a record of which alarms are currently scheduled so they can be kept in step with your tasks.

Information the app does not collect

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third party trackers and no code that sends your content anywhere. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or calendar. There is no account to create and nothing to log in to.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone. Nothing is sent through a push server, so no remote service learns what your tasks are called or when they are due.

Nag Me! uses the iOS alarm system so an urgent task can sound at its due time even in silent mode. That also runs entirely on your device. Both notifications and alarms ask your permission first, and you can withdraw either at any time in iOS Settings. Declining them does not stop the rest of the app from working.

Tips and the App Store

The app offers three optional tips as In-App Purchases. They are entirely voluntary and unlock nothing: the app behaves identically whether or not you ever send one.

If you choose to send a tip, Apple handles the entire transaction. Your payment details go to Apple, never to this app or to the developer, and the app does not receive or store your card details, billing address or Apple Account information. The developer receives only the aggregate sales reports Apple provides in App Store Connect, which do not identify individual customers. Apple's own privacy policy governs that part.

The Settings screen also offers to open Apple's rating prompt or the App Store review page. Both are handled by Apple, and the app is not told whether you rated it or what you said.

Exporting your own data

Settings has an Export and Backup option that creates a JSON and a CSV copy of your tasks and attaches them to an email addressed to you. This only happens when you ask for it, the message is composed in your own mail app, and you decide whether to send it and to whom. The developer does not receive a copy.

Children

The app is a general audience to-do list. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays on your iPhone for as long as the app is installed. Delete a task to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything it stored along with any reminders it had scheduled. Because nothing was ever uploaded, there is no copy elsewhere for the developer to erase and no request you need to send. See Delete your data for the steps.

One thing sits outside the app's control: if you have an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, that backup may still contain the app's data until it is replaced or deleted in your iOS or iCloud settings. The same applies to any export file you emailed to yourself.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it. Material changes will also be noted in the changelog.

Contact

Write to ziadmohofficial@gmail.com with any privacy question. Messages are usually answered within a few days.
