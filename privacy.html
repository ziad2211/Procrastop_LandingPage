Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

About this policy

Procrastop is provided by Ziad Ismail.

Information we collect

Procrastop is designed to work without requiring an account or login. The app does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password. The tasks and other content you create in Procrastop are stored locally on your device and are not collected by or transmitted to the developer through the app.

Procrastop may request permission to send notifications so that it can remind you about your tasks. Notification permission is controlled by you through your device settings. Procrastop does not use notifications to collect personal information.

Procrastop does not require access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or other personal device information for its core functionality.

How we use and share information

Because Procrastop does not require an account and does not collect your task data on developer-operated servers, your task information is not used for advertising, sold to third parties, or shared by the developer with third parties.

Procrastop does not intentionally collect analytics or tracking data that identifies you. The app may rely on services provided by Apple as part of the normal operation and distribution of an iOS application. Those services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Your information remains on your device unless you choose to use an iOS feature or another service that causes information to be shared outside the app. Procrastop does not operate a separate account database containing your personal information.

Your choices and account deletion

You can control Procrastop's notification permission through your device's settings. Because Procrastop does not require user accounts, there is no Procrastop account that you need to delete. You can delete your tasks and other locally stored app data using the functionality provided within the app or by deleting Procrastop from your device, subject to how iOS handles locally stored and backed-up application data.
