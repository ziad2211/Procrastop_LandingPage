Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

About this policy

Procrastop is an iPhone to-do list app published by Ziad Ismail. This policy describes how the app handles information. It covers the Procrastop iOS app and this website.

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date above.

Information we collect

Procrastop does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or a password, and it does not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

The tasks you create — their titles, due dates and times, notes, repeat settings, stars, and completion history — together with your app settings are stored only in Procrastop's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer, and Procrastop does not operate a server, account database, or cloud sync that could receive them.

Procrastop asks iOS for permission to send notifications, which it uses for local reminders about your own tasks and for the app icon badge. These notifications are scheduled and delivered on your device by iOS. You can grant or revoke the permission at any time in the iOS Settings app; without it, the app still works, but reminders and the badge do not appear.

Procrastop does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, calendar, microphone, camera, or health data, and it does not use the network. It has no in-app purchases and does not process payments.

How we use and share information

Because your tasks never leave your device through the app, there is nothing for the developer to use, sell, or share. Your information is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not disclosed to third parties.

One feature lets you choose to send your own data somewhere, and it is started by you and handled entirely by iOS:

  • Export and backup. Settings offers a backup of your tasks as JSON and CSV files, attached to a draft email in the iOS Mail composer addressed to you, or passed to the share sheet if Mail is not set up. The draft is not sent until you send it, and it is not copied to the developer.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives whatever you include in your message and uses it only to answer you.

Procrastop is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about downloads, crashes, and purchases independently of the app, under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer receives only the aggregate reports Apple provides.

Your choices and account deletion

Because Procrastop has no accounts, there is no account to delete and no personal data held by the developer to request, correct, or erase. You are in control of everything the app stores:

  • Delete individual tasks inside the app.
  • Turn notifications on or off in iOS Settings › Notifications › Procrastop.
  • Delete the app from your iPhone to remove its stored tasks and settings. A copy may remain in an iCloud or computer backup you made earlier, until that backup is replaced or deleted.

See Delete your data for the full steps.

Procrastop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
