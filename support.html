Support

Need help with Procrastop? Support is handled by the developer, Ziad Ismail, by email.

Email ziadmohofficial@gmail.com. Messages are usually answered within a few days.

Reporting a problem

So an issue can be reproduced, include the app version from Settings › About, your iPhone model, and your iOS version, plus what you did and what happened instead.

If tasks are involved, Settings › Export / Backup can attach a JSON and CSV copy of your list to an email. Send it only if you are comfortable sharing those task titles; it is never needed for a first reply.

Things worth checking first

If reminders are not arriving, confirm that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings › Notifications › Procrastop, and that the task has a due time set. If a task seems to be in the wrong section, check the day ranges in Settings › Change Term Lengths — those decide when a task becomes Short-Term, Mid-Term, or Long-Term. Most other questions are answered in the FAQ.

Feature requests and feedback

Procrastop is deliberately small: due dates, three timeframes, and reminders, with no projects, tags, calendar sync, or accounts. Suggestions are still welcome at the same address.

Privacy requests

Procrastop has no accounts and stores your tasks only on your iPhone, so there is no stored personal data to export or erase; see the Privacy Policy and Delete your data. For anything else, write to the same address with the subject line “Privacy request”.
