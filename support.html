Support

Need help with Procrastop? Support is handled by the developer, Ziad Ismail, by email.

Write to ziadmohofficial@gmail.com. Messages are usually answered within a few days.

Things worth checking first

Reminders are not arriving. Confirm notifications are allowed in iOS Settings, Notifications, Procrastop, and that the task actually has a due time set. A task with only a date gets the midnight nudge, not a timed reminder.

The Nag Me! alarm is not sounding. Nag Me! uses the system alarm feature, which needs its own permission and is available on iPhones running iOS 26 or later. Check that alarms are allowed for Procrastop in iOS Settings, and that the switch is on both for the task and in Settings, Notification Options.

A task is in the wrong section. The day ranges in Settings, Change Term Lengths decide when a task becomes Short-Term, Mid-Term or Long-Term.

Most other questions are answered in the FAQ.

Reporting a problem

So an issue can be reproduced, include the app version from Settings, About, your iPhone model, and your iOS version, plus what you did and what happened instead.

If tasks are involved, Settings, Export and Backup can attach a JSON and CSV copy of your list to an email. Send it only if you are comfortable sharing those task titles. It is never needed for a first reply.

Feature requests and feedback

Procrastop is deliberately small: due dates, three timeframes and reminders, with no projects, tags, calendar sync or accounts. Suggestions are still welcome at the same address.

Tips and purchases

The optional tips in Settings are In-App Purchases handled entirely by Apple. If a purchase does not go through, or you want a refund, that is between you and Apple through its usual process. Since a tip unlocks nothing, nothing in the app needs restoring afterwards.

Privacy requests

Procrastop has no accounts and stores your tasks only on your iPhone, so there is no stored personal data to export or erase. See the Privacy Policy and Delete your data. For anything else, write to the same address with the subject line Privacy request.
